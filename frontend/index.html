About Dr. Enriquez

📚

Academic Background

Dr. Enrico L. Enriquez is a Full Professor in the Department of Computer Information Sciences and Mathematics at the University of San Carlos, Cebu City. He specializes in graph theory with a focus on domination in graphs, studying binary operations between connected nontrivial graphs.

Research Interests

  • Graph Theory & Domination in Graphs
  • Real Analysis
  • Topology
  • Binary operations on graphs (join, corona, lexicographic product, Cartesian product)
📋

Current Work

Dr. Enriquez is currently mentoring graduate students on their theses in the area of Domination in Graphs. His research program focuses on characterizing special dominating sets in graph products and operations, contributing foundational results to combinatorics and discrete mathematics.
